The Andromeda shift

The ad is now
the targeting.

Meta's delivery system is getting better at deciding who should see which ad. That changes the job for brands. When the platform can do more of the audience finding, creative has to do more of the selling.

01 / The problem

One winning ad is not a strategy.

Brands that keep recycling the same hook, promise and visual eventually pay for it. Repetition creates fatigue. Weak differentiation makes every new ad feel like the last one.

Creative fatigueSame message. Same audience. Less response.
